Where the jobs are, and how flexible they are

We cleaned and analysed 1,095 online job postings across six tech and business roles to see where employers hire and how many roles can be done remotely.

Key findings

Over half of all postings are remote or hybrid, and the six roles are almost equally represented.

1,095job postings analysed
62countries with a known location
232cities identified
52.5%remote or hybrid (575 postings)

Six roles, evenly matched

UI/UX has the most postings (191) and Cloud the fewest (170). The standard deviation across roles is only 8.06 postings.

Which roles are most flexible

Share of postings that are remote or hybrid. The orange line marks the overall average of 52.5%.

UI/UX stands out: 107 of its 191 postings are fully remote. HR is the most on-site role.

Top ten countries

The United States, United Kingdom and India account for 497 of the 1,027 postings with a known country.

Volume does not predict flexibility

For the 21 countries with at least 10 postings, the correlation between number of postings and remote-friendly share is 0.03, which is practically none. The Philippines is 75.5% remote-friendly, the UK 60.5%, the US 59.3% and India 44.6%.
